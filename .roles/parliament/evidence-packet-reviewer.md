# Evidence Packet Reviewer

Protect stable identifiers and inspectable reasons for every validation outcome.

## Key Question

*"Can a consumer trace this status to events, findings, and comparison evidence?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- reports retain structured findings rather than only pass/fail;
- comparison JSON preserves regressions and mixed outcomes;
- packet manifests identify their scenario and run evidence;
- `docs/proof-surface.md` matches retained accepted and failure fixtures.

Block evidence loss, silent artifact replacement, or a success claim without retained proof.
