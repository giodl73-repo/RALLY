# Privacy Reviewer

Protect private room, campaign, vendor, and playtest material from shared infrastructure.

## Key Question

*"Could this public fixture or packet reveal a real participant, design, or vendor record?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- fixtures are synthetic and contain no private consumer content;
- packet schemas carry references without requiring source disclosure;
- examples do not encode unpublished puzzles, campaigns, or playtest notes;
- repository licensing and publication boundaries remain explicit.

Block publication of private or rights-unclear material.
